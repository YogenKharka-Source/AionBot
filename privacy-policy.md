# Privacy Policy

**AionBot** ("the Bot", "we", "us")
**Last updated:** October 13, 2026

AionBot is a Discord bot for Aion 2 communities. It posts reset and Spacetime Rift alerts, shares official game news, gives members a private daily and weekly checklist, and offers a role panel for notification roles. This policy explains what information the Bot handles, why, and the choices you have.

**In short:** the Bot stores only the Discord IDs and settings it needs to work. It does not read your messages, does not sell data, and does not use data for advertising.

## 1. Information We Collect and Store

The Bot saves the following in files on the computer or server where it runs.

| Feature | What is stored | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Server setup (all features) | Server (guild) ID, the channel and role IDs chosen by server admins, and the IDs of the Bot's own previous alert messages | To post alerts in the right place and replace older alerts |
| Reset and rift alerts | Alert channel and role IDs, and the rift schedule alignment if an admin runs `/riftsync` | To send alerts on schedule |
| Official news | News channel ID, optional ping role ID, and the IDs of Steam news posts already shared | To post each news item once |
| Private checklist | Your Discord user ID, which daily and weekly tasks you have ticked, the ID of your private checklist thread, and the checklist channel ID | To save your progress and reopen your thread |
| Role panel | The roles on the panel (role ID, button label, description) and the panel message ID | To show the panel and keep its buttons working |

**Roles you choose.** When you press a role panel button, the Bot adds or removes that role on your account. Discord itself records your roles. The Bot does not keep its own list of who holds which role. It may briefly keep a reference to your private confirmation message in memory so it can delete your previous one.

**Commands.** When you use a command or button, Discord sends the Bot details such as your user ID, the server and channel, and any options you entered. The Bot uses these only to respond, and saves only what is listed in the table above.

## 2. Information We Do Not Collect

- The content of messages. The Bot does not use Discord's privileged Message Content, Server Members, or Presence intents.
- Direct messages, voice data, email addresses, passwords, payment details, IP addresses, or your location.
- Your time zone. Times in alerts use Discord's built-in timestamps, which Discord displays in your own local time.

The Bot has permission to read message history in channels where it posts, but only to find and delete its own earlier alerts. It does not store or analyze other people's messages.

## 3. How We Use Information

We use the stored information only to:
- Run the Bot's features (alerts, news, checklist, role panel).
- Save server settings and your checklist progress.
- Diagnose errors and keep the Bot working.

We do not use it for advertising, profiling, or any unrelated purpose.

## 4. Private Checklist Threads

Each checklist is a private Discord thread. It is visible to you, the Bot, and any server members whose permissions let them see all private threads (such as Administrator or Manage Threads). This is a Discord feature, not something the Bot controls. The thread contains only your task checklist.

## 5. Third Parties

- **Discord.** The Bot runs on Discord, which handles your account and messages under [Discord's Privacy Policy](https://discord.com/privacy).
- **Steam.** To share official news, the Bot reads Steam's public news feed for the game. No personal information about you or your server is sent to Steam.
- **Hosting.** Data is stored on the computer or server where the Bot runs, which is controlled by the Bot's operator. We do not use third-party analytics or advertising services.

We do not sell or rent your information. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the safety of users.

## 6. How Long We Keep Information

- **Server settings** are kept while the Bot is in your server, until a server admin asks us to delete them.
- **Checklist progress** (ticked tasks) resets automatically at the daily and weekly game resets. The record linking your user ID to your checklist thread is kept until you ask us to delete it.
- **Posted news IDs and panel settings** are kept for as long as the feature is in use in the server.
- **Backups**, if any, are deleted on the same schedule when we remove data on request.

## 7. Your Choices and Rights

- Close your checklist at any time with the **Close checklist** button. Your progress stays saved until the next reset.
- Add or remove notification roles yourself on the role panel at any time.
- Server admins can stop any feature by removing the Bot from the server or changing its channels and roles.
- You can ask what data we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by contacting **[CONTACT EMAIL]** with your Discord user ID. Server owners can request deletion of their server's data the same way. We will respond within a reasonable time, generally within 30 days.

## 8. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect stored data, including keeping the Bot's login token private and giving the Bot only the permissions it needs (it does not use Administrator). No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 9. Children

The Bot is not intended for anyone under the minimum age required to use Discord. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under that age. If you believe we have, contact us and we will delete it.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above will change when we do. Continued use of the Bot after a change means you accept the updated policy.
